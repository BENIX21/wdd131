3Demente

Site Name

Represents custom 3D modeling, rapid prototyping, and on-demand 3D printed parts with a creative, engineering-focused identity.

Optional domain: your3dbrand.com

Site Purpose

The website serves as a showcase and ordering hub for custom 3D printing services. It presents the catalog of available prints and materials, explains how custom orders work, and shares background about the studio.

Scenarios

  • Which 3D printing filaments or materials are best for functional parts versus display models?
  • How do I submit a custom 3D print request and get an estimate?
  • Where can I view examples of completed 3D prints and learn about the studio?

Color Schema

#040C19

Header, footer, dark accents, and body text on light backgrounds

#00A0C8

Accent borders, highlights, and accents over #040C19

#FFFFFF

Main page background and text over #040C19

Typography

Orbitron (sans-serif display) — used for main headings (h1, h2) to give a technical, 3D-inspired look.

Plus Jakarta Sans (sans-serif) — used for body copy, lists, and labels for clean readability across screens.
